What is the Dalston LTN?

A low traffic neighbourhood between the A10, Dalston Lane and Amhurst Road: a 24-hour bus gate on Shacklewell Lane, filters on Foulden, Farleigh, Arcola and Cecilia Roads, turn bans and two School Streets. The aim is to stop cut-through traffic on residential streets, which is a fair goal.

The problem: that traffic moves onto Dalston Lane, Amhurst Road, the A10 and Graham Road, where the Council itself describes a "traffic standstill at peak times". The Pembury Circus works have only just finished, and officers say they won't have the data to assess them until December.

Our ask: get the data first. Defer these orders until the impact on Hackney Central is known and mitigation is in place.
